How does a suspension change bike geometry?
  1. Cane Creek Invert has two models: 30 mm (425 mm axle-crown) and 40 (435 mm axle-crown) mm travel. Both models have 45 mm offset
  2. Rockshox Rudy has four models: 30 mm (425 mm axle-crown) and 40 (435 mm axle-crown) mm travel, each with either 45 mm or 51 mm offset.
  3. Fox 32 TC Factory has four models: 40 (435.5 mm axle-crown) mm and 50 (445.5 mm axle-crown) mm travel, each with either 45 mm or 50 mm offset.
Note
  1. The suspension fork length is based on 10% sag: fork_length - 0.1*travel
